# Fast Punchy

## Purpose
Create rapid, hard-hitting rap verses built from compact bars, immediate impact, and frequent memorable punchlines.

## Style Lock
When this style is selected, **do not merely imitate the mood**. The generated lyrics must visibly satisfy the structural and linguistic rules below. If the draft fails the self-check, rewrite it before returning the final lyrics.

## Core Profile
- **Energy:** Very high
- **Delivery Speed:** Fast / double-time capable
- **Target Syllables:** 8–13 syllables per bar, with occasional shorter impact bars
- **Rhyme Density:** High
- **Internal Rhyme Density:** Medium-high
- **Multisyllabic Rhyme Level:** Medium
- **Punchline Frequency:** At least 1 strong punchline or payoff every 2 bars
- **Wordplay Level:** High
- **Metaphor Density:** Medium-high
- **Vocabulary:** Direct, vivid, compact
- **Storytelling Level:** Low-medium
- **Aggression:** High
- **Emotional Intensity:** Medium-high
- **Repetition:** Low outside hooks
- **Flow Variation:** Frequent micro-switches without losing momentum
- **Preferred Rhyme Schemes:** AA/BB, AAAA bursts, internal chains
- **Bar Structure:** Short clauses, hard stops, minimal setup time
- **Hook Compatibility:** High

## Mandatory Writing Rules
- Open with impact within the first 2 bars.
- Keep most bars compact enough for fast delivery.
- Use strong stressed rhyme words at line endings.
- Include internal rhymes in at least half the bars.
- Deliver a payoff, flip, boast, image, or punchline at least every 2 bars.
- Use verbs and concrete nouns more than vague adjectives.
- Keep transitions fast; avoid long narrative detours.

## Avoid
Long explanations, filler bars, soft openings, overly complex sentence structures that slow delivery

## Generation Procedure
1. Decide the section purpose before writing: verse, hook, bridge, intro, or outro.
2. Establish a rhythmic pocket that matches this style.
3. Draft the lyrics using the target syllable range and rhyme behavior above.
4. Strengthen weak end words, internal rhyme positions, and rhythmic accents.
5. Remove filler and any line that does not support the style objective.
6. Run the **Style Compliance Check** below.
7. Rewrite failed lines before producing the final result.

## Style Compliance Check
- [ ] Does the verse feel fast even when read silently?
- [ ] Are at least 50% of bars carrying internal rhyme or rhythmic echo?
- [ ] Is there a memorable impact line at least every 2 bars?
- [ ] Could any bar be shortened without losing meaning? If yes, tighten it.
- [ ] Are filler connectors removed?

## Failure Conditions
The output should be considered **off-style** and regenerated if:
- The defining cadence or rhyme behavior is missing.
- More than roughly 25% of the bars ignore the target structure.
- Filler lines are used to reach length.
- The vocabulary, emotional tone, or rhyme density drifts into a different preset.
- The generator describes the style instead of actually writing in it.

## App Instruction
When this preset is active, treat these rules as a **hard style constraint**. Content prompts may change the subject, setting, story, mood, or perspective, but they should not override the core rhythmic and lyrical behavior unless the user explicitly disables this style.

## Combination Behavior
If combined with another preset:
- Preserve this style's strongest defining traits.
- Merge compatible attributes rather than averaging everything into a generic midpoint.
- If two rules conflict, prioritize the user's explicitly selected primary style.
- Never allow combination mode to remove all defining features of this preset.
