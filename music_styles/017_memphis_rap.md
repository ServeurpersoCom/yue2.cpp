# Memphis Rap

**Family:** Southern / Dark
**Typical tempo:** 70–95 BPM

## Core Identity

Dark, hypnotic Southern rap rooted in raw drum-machine patterns, eerie samples, repetitive motifs, and lo-fi menace.

## Rhythm & Drums

Dry drum-machine hits, sharp snares, rapid hats, repetitive patterns.

## Bass & Low End

Distorted or simple sub-bass; low end can feel rough and narrow.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Horror-film samples, minor piano, cowbells, vocal chops, tape degradation.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Repetitive, incantatory, laid-back or menacing flows.

## Arrangement

Short loops, sparse arrangement, recurring vocal/sample motifs.

## Mood & Energy

Sinister, hypnotic, nocturnal, raw.
