# Psychedelic Hip-Hop

**Family:** Psychedelic / Experimental
**Typical tempo:** 65–110 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop with altered, dreamlike sound design: warped samples, phase movement, unusual stereo space, surreal harmony, and evolving textures.

## Rhythm & Drums

Groove can be laid-back or broken; use filtered and shifting percussion.

## Bass & Low End

Warm bass or sub with modulation and occasional pitch movement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Reversed samples, phaser/flanger, tape warble, synth drones, exotic timbres.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Elastic, hypnotic, melodic, abstract, or heavily layered vocals.

## Arrangement

Evolving loops, transitions that morph rather than simply cut.

## Mood & Energy

Surreal, immersive, colorful, disorienting.

## Performance Detail

Maintain one recognizable anchor—groove, motif, bass figure, or vocal rhythm—while other elements mutate around it.
