# Jazz Rap

**Family:** Jazz / Soul
**Typical tempo:** 78–100 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop built around jazz harmony, improvisational textures, upright or electric bass, and articulate rhythmic rapping.

## Rhythm & Drums

Boom-bap or brushed grooves; syncopated percussion can add live feel.

## Bass & Low End

Walking or melodic basslines, often more active than standard hip-hop.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Extended chords, sax, trumpet, Rhodes, vibraphone, piano, jazz guitar.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Conversational, intelligent, elastic flow; leave space for instrumentation.

## Arrangement

Verse/hook form with instrumental interludes, solos, or evolving comping.

## Mood & Energy

Sophisticated, cool, reflective, urban.
