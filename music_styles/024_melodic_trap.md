# Melodic Trap

**Family:** Trap / Melodic
**Typical tempo:** 120–155 BPM

## Core Identity

Trap production shaped around memorable melodies, emotional chord progressions, sung-rap phrasing, and smooth 808 movement.

## Rhythm & Drums

Cleaner trap drums with less abrasive attack.

## Bass & Low End

Warm or gliding 808 that follows harmonic movement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Guitar, piano, plucks, pads, airy synths, emotional motifs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Sung-rap, harmonized hooks, flexible pitch, intimate delivery.

## Arrangement

Hook-centric songwriting, bridges, vocal layers, instrumental lifts.

## Mood & Energy

Emotional, atmospheric, polished, bittersweet.

## Performance Detail

Treat the 808 as both bass and rhythmic counterpoint; leave intentional gaps so slides and drum accents remain clear.
