# Lo-Fi Hip-Hop

**Family:** Lo-Fi / Chill
**Typical tempo:** 65–90 BPM

## Core Identity

Dusty, intimate hip-hop with softened transients, tape/vinyl wear, mellow harmony, and relaxed repetition.

## Rhythm & Drums

Soft kicks, brushed or muted snares, lazy hats, noticeable swing.

## Bass & Low End

Rounded bass with limited sub aggression.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Jazz chords, Rhodes, guitar, piano, vinyl noise, wow/flutter, filtered samples.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Usually restrained rap, spoken phrases, or instrumental focus.

## Arrangement

Short loop cycles with gentle variation; avoid dramatic drops.

## Mood & Energy

Warm, nostalgic, calm, late-night.

## Performance Detail

Use degradation selectively. The mix can sound worn without becoming muddy; preserve a readable kick, snare, and vocal range.
