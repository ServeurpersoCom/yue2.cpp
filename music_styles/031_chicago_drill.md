# Chicago Drill

**Family:** Drill / Regional
**Typical tempo:** 130–150 BPM

## Core Identity

Early drill aesthetic with raw minimalism, stark street energy, repetitive melodic loops, and blunt rhythmic force.

## Rhythm & Drums

Simple hard drums, rolling hats, direct clap/snare patterns.

## Bass & Low End

Heavy 808s, less ornate than later UK-style sliding bass.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Dark synths, bells, simple minor motifs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Direct, repetitive, forceful, often less technically busy.

## Arrangement

Long verses, repeated hooks, deliberately minimal development.

## Mood & Energy

Raw, stark, aggressive, bleak.

## Performance Detail

The kick and 808 should interlock rather than constantly hit together; bass slides need deliberate pitch targets.
