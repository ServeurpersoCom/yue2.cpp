# Plugg

**Family:** Trap / Internet
**Typical tempo:** 125–155 BPM

## Core Identity

Bright, minimal internet-era rap production with playful synths, light drum bounce, and simple catchy motifs.

## Rhythm & Drums

Light trap drums, crisp claps, airy hats, bouncing percussion.

## Bass & Low End

Clean 808s, usually less distorted and more buoyant.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Bell-like leads, digital plucks, bright synths, simple arpeggios.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Relaxed, playful, melodic or conversational delivery.

## Arrangement

Short sections, repetitive hooks, minimal layering.

## Mood & Energy

Playful, airy, digital, effortless.

## Performance Detail

Treat the 808 as both bass and rhythmic counterpoint; leave intentional gaps so slides and drum accents remain clear.
