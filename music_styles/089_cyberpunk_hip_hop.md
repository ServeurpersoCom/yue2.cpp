# Cyberpunk Hip-Hop

**Family:** Futuristic / Dark
**Typical tempo:** 75–150 BPM

## Core Identity

Dark futuristic rap combining industrial textures, neon synths, distorted bass, machine rhythms, and dystopian atmosphere.

## Rhythm & Drums

Mechanical drums, hard transients, glitch details.

## Bass & Low End

Distorted synth bass/808, sub drones.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Dark synthwave leads, noise, alarms, granular effects, metallic ambience.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Cold, processed, aggressive, spoken or melodic delivery.

## Arrangement

Cinematic intro, heavy drop, breakdowns with machine ambience.

## Mood & Energy

Dystopian, neon-lit, tense, technological.
