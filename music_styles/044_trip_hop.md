# Trip-Hop

**Family:** Downtempo / Atmospheric
**Typical tempo:** 60–95 BPM

## Core Identity

Slow, moody breakbeat music blending hip-hop rhythm with cinematic ambience, dub space, and dark melodic texture.

## Rhythm & Drums

Slow breakbeats, dusty snares, deep pocket, occasional live percussion.

## Bass & Low End

Warm sub, dub bass, or melodic low-end loop.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Moody strings, Rhodes, guitar, vinyl texture, atmospheric samples.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Low-key rap, spoken word, breathy singing, or instrumental focus.

## Arrangement

Long intros/outros, cinematic builds, restrained hooks.

## Mood & Energy

Smoky, nocturnal, cinematic, introspective.
