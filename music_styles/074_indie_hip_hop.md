# Indie Hip-Hop

**Family:** Underground / Alternative
**Typical tempo:** 65–115 BPM

## Core Identity

Artist-driven hip-hop with idiosyncratic production, unconventional influences, and less concern for mainstream structure.

## Rhythm & Drums

Varies widely; should feel intentional and characterful.

## Bass & Low End

Bass chosen for song identity, not genre convention.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Indie rock, jazz, electronics, folk, soul, or experimental elements.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Distinctive personal voice; can mix rap and singing.

## Arrangement

Flexible structures, unexpected bridges, instrumental passages.

## Mood & Energy

Individualistic, creative, authentic, exploratory.
