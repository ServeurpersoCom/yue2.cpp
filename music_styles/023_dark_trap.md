# Dark Trap

**Family:** Trap / Dark
**Typical tempo:** 120–155 BPM

## Core Identity

Trap with ominous harmony, cinematic tension, distorted low end, and sparse threatening sound design.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hard transient drums, controlled hat rolls, dramatic pauses.

## Bass & Low End

Heavy distorted 808s, slides, sustained sub pressure.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Minor/Phrygian colors, bells, choirs, reversed textures, dark pads.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Low-register, restrained, threatening or emotionally cold delivery.

## Arrangement

Tension-building intros, hard drops, sparse verses, dramatic transitions.

## Mood & Energy

Menacing, nocturnal, cinematic, severe.

## Performance Detail

Treat the 808 as both bass and rhythmic counterpoint; leave intentional gaps so slides and drum accents remain clear.
