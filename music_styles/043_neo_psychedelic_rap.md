# Neo-Psychedelic Rap

**Family:** Psychedelic / Modern
**Typical tempo:** 110–155 BPM

## Core Identity

Modern psychedelic rap combining trap or alternative drums with lush synth worlds, modulation, reverbs, and cinematic movement.

## Rhythm & Drums

Trap-informed drums with spacious gaps and textural effects.

## Bass & Low End

Gliding 808 or warm sub integrated into atmospheric mix.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Wide pads, detuned synths, reversed guitars, granular vocal textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Melodic rap, layered harmonies, ad-libs, processed vocal space.

## Arrangement

Hook-centered but with evolving sonic transitions.

## Mood & Energy

Dreamlike, futuristic, emotionally charged.

## Performance Detail

Maintain one recognizable anchor—groove, motif, bass figure, or vocal rhythm—while other elements mutate around it.
