# Emo Rap

**Family:** Melodic / Emotional
**Typical tempo:** 115–155 BPM or slow half-time

## Core Identity

Emotionally vulnerable rap combining trap/lo-fi production with melodic vocals, guitar or piano motifs, and confessional writing.

## Rhythm & Drums

Soft trap drums, restrained hats, emotional pauses.

## Bass & Low End

Warm 808, often following chord roots.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Guitar loops, piano, pads, vocal texture, minor/relative-major harmony.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Melodic rap, fragile singing, layered doubles, intimate delivery.

## Arrangement

Hook-heavy, compact verses, bridge or breakdown for emotional peak.

## Mood & Energy

Vulnerable, melancholic, intimate, cathartic.
