# Jazz Boom Bap

**Family:** Boom Bap / Jazz
**Typical tempo:** 78–96 BPM

## Core Identity

Classic boom-bap drums paired with jazz samples, extended harmony, horn fragments, and swinging musical phrasing.

## Rhythm & Drums

Dusty kick/snare, swing, occasional brushed percussion.

## Bass & Low End

Upright/electric bass loops, walking fragments.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Sax, trumpet, piano, vibraphone, Rhodes, jazz guitar.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Relaxed but articulate, pocket-aware lyrical flow.

## Arrangement

Sample loop evolves through horn/piano variations and cutaways.

## Mood & Energy

Cool, sophisticated, smoky, head-nod.

## Performance Detail

Keep the groove human: slight swing and imperfect sample timing are usually more convincing than rigid quantization.
