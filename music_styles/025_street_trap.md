# Street Trap

**Family:** Trap / Street
**Typical tempo:** 125–155 BPM

## Core Identity

Hard, minimal trap focused on immediacy, street narrative, aggressive low end, and direct vocal presence.

## Rhythm & Drums

Dry punchy drums, sharp hats, sparse fills.

## Bass & Low End

Dominant 808, often short and punchy with occasional slides.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Minimal piano, bells, dark pads, simple repeating motifs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Direct, rhythmically assertive, less melodic than melodic trap.

## Arrangement

Strong hooks, stripped verses, occasional beat cuts for emphasis.

## Mood & Energy

Cold, hard, direct, tense.

## Performance Detail

Treat the 808 as both bass and rhythmic counterpoint; leave intentional gaps so slides and drum accents remain clear.
