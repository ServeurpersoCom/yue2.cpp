# Underground Hip-Hop

**Family:** Underground / Alternative
**Typical tempo:** 75–96 BPM

## Core Identity

Independent-minded hip-hop prioritizing character, lyrical depth, unusual samples, and less-polished textures over mainstream formulas.

## Rhythm & Drums

Dry, gritty, sometimes deliberately uneven; drums can be sparse or unusually layered.

## Bass & Low End

Muted sub, sampled bass, or dirty low-end loop.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Obscure samples, degraded textures, eerie keys, dusty jazz, field recordings, unconventional chops.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Dense writing, abstract imagery, technical or introspective delivery.

## Arrangement

Longer verses, less predictable hooks, beat switches or sample evolutions.

## Mood & Energy

Raw, cerebral, personal, uncompromising.
