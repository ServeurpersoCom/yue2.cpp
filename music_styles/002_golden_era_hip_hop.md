# Golden Era Hip-Hop

**Family:** Classic / Sample-Based
**Typical tempo:** 88–108 BPM

## Core Identity

Late-1980s to early-1990s hip-hop language: breakbeats, funk-derived samples, direct MC performance, DJ culture, and memorable rhythmic hooks.

## Rhythm & Drums

Breakbeat-led, lively hats, snare-forward backbeat, occasional percussion loops and cuts.

## Bass & Low End

Funk or sampled basslines with clear movement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Funk, soul, jazz, horn hits, scratches, vocal snippets, simple melodic loops.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Direct, rhythmic, charismatic; call-and-response and crew energy work well.

## Arrangement

Clear intro/verse/hook structure with DJ breaks and short transitions.

## Mood & Energy

Energetic, streetwise, celebratory, foundational.
