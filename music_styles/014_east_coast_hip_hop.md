# East Coast Hip-Hop

**Family:** Regional
**Typical tempo:** 78–98 BPM

## Core Identity

Dense, rhythm-first hip-hop associated with chopped samples, hard drums, lyrical emphasis, and gritty urban atmosphere.

## Rhythm & Drums

Punchy boom-bap, sharp snares, compact swing, layered breaks.

## Bass & Low End

Sampled bass, dark low-end loops, controlled sub.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Soul/jazz chops, piano stabs, horns, strings, eerie samples.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Bar-heavy delivery with tight rhyme placement.

## Arrangement

Loop-focused verses, concise hooks, DJ cuts or sample drops.

## Mood & Energy

Gritty, focused, nocturnal, lyrical.

## Performance Detail

Keep the groove human: slight swing and imperfect sample timing are usually more convincing than rigid quantization.
