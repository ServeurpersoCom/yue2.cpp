# Lo-Fi Underground

**Family:** Underground / Lo-Fi
**Typical tempo:** 70–92 BPM

## Core Identity

Raw underground rap with degraded sample texture, dusty drums, narrow bandwidth, and deliberately unpolished sonics.

## Rhythm & Drums

Crunchy, clipped, dusty, slightly loose.

## Bass & Low End

Sampled bass or muted sub.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Obscure chops, tape hiss, vinyl crackle, filtered loops.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Low-key, dense, conversational, often dry and close.

## Arrangement

Loop-heavy, subtle changes, short hooks or none.

## Mood & Energy

Gritty, intimate, obscure, late-night.

## Performance Detail

Use degradation selectively. The mix can sound worn without becoming muddy; preserve a readable kick, snare, and vocal range.
