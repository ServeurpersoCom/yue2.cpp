# Houston Rap

**Family:** Southern / Regional
**Typical tempo:** 60–85 BPM

## Core Identity

Slow, spacious Southern hip-hop with deep bass, relaxed cadence, soulful atmosphere, and room for chopped-and-screwed influence.

## Rhythm & Drums

Slow rolling drums, crisp claps, loose hats.

## Bass & Low End

Deep sustained sub, warm bass, occasionally melodic slides.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Soulful samples, synth pads, piano, syrupy textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Drawn-out cadence, conversational flow, melodic phrases.

## Arrangement

Long grooves and spacious sections; hooks can stretch.

## Mood & Energy

Heavy, smooth, late-night, luxurious.
