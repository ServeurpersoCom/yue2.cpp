# Chipmunk Soul

**Family:** Soul / Sample-Based
**Typical tempo:** 80–100 BPM

## Core Identity

Soul-sampling style built around noticeably pitch-raised vocal samples, energetic chops, and bright emotional contrast against hard drums.

## Rhythm & Drums

Punchy boom-bap or early-2000s-style drums.

## Bass & Low End

Simple bass supporting sample roots.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

High-pitched soul vocal phrases, chopped hooks, strings, piano, gospel fragments.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Confident rap with room for sample call-and-response.

## Arrangement

Strong sampled hook, verses with reduced sample density, dramatic chops.

## Mood & Energy

Triumphant, nostalgic, emotional, energetic.
