# Korean Hip-Hop

**Family:** Regional / Asian
**Typical tempo:** 70–160 BPM

## Core Identity

Modern Korean hip-hop spanning boom-bap, trap, R&B hybrids, glossy electronic production, and technical rap performance.

## Rhythm & Drums

Clean, punchy modern drums; style-dependent.

## Bass & Low End

Controlled 808/sub, often polished and balanced.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Trap synths, R&B chords, boom-bap samples, electronic textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Technically precise rap, melodic hooks, rapid switches between rap and singing.

## Arrangement

Polished song structure with contrast sections and strong hooks.

## Mood & Energy

Polished, energetic, stylish, versatile.
