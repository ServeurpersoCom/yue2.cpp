# Space Rap

**Family:** Futuristic / Atmospheric
**Typical tempo:** 70–150 BPM

## Core Identity

Futuristic hip-hop built around cosmic synth design, spacious stereo imaging, sci-fi ambience, and floating low end.

## Rhythm & Drums

Electronic drums with clean transients and wide spatial effects.

## Bass & Low End

Deep sub or gliding 808, sometimes modulated.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Arps, sci-fi pads, FM tones, granular effects, orbital ambience.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Cool, detached, melodic or technically precise rap.

## Arrangement

Expansive intros, drop-based verses, atmospheric bridges.

## Mood & Energy

Cosmic, futuristic, isolated, expansive.
