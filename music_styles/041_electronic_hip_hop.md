# Electronic Hip-Hop

**Family:** Electronic
**Typical tempo:** 75–150 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop built primarily from synthesis, drum machines, and electronic sound design rather than sample-based production.

## Rhythm & Drums

Programmed drums, synthetic percussion, precise transients.

## Bass & Low End

Sub bass, synth bass, or 808 depending substyle.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Synth leads, pads, arps, electronic effects, digital texture.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Any rap approach; often clean and rhythmically tight.

## Arrangement

Song structure can borrow from hip-hop or electronic music.

## Mood & Energy

Modern, synthetic, polished, flexible.
