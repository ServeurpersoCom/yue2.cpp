# Bounce

**Family:** Southern / Club
**Typical tempo:** 95–115 BPM

## Core Identity

New Orleans-rooted dance rap with fast, repetitive rhythms, call-and-response vocals, and syncopated party energy.

## Rhythm & Drums

Rapid kick patterns, claps, repetitive percussion loops.

## Bass & Low End

Simple sub or bass pulse supporting the rhythm.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Minimal melodic loops, vocal samples, whistles, bright stabs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Call-and-response, chants, repeated phrases, energetic MCing.

## Arrangement

Loop-driven, repetitive, built for continuous movement.

## Mood & Energy

Celebratory, kinetic, playful, high-energy.

## Performance Detail

Prioritize physical rhythm and fast recognition. Hooks should arrive quickly and low-end transients should remain uncluttered.
