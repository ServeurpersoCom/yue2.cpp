# Freestyle Rap

**Family:** Performance / Lyrical
**Typical tempo:** 80–100 BPM

## Core Identity

Beat design optimized for spontaneous MCing: clear pocket, predictable phrase lengths, restrained arrangement, and strong loop continuity.

## Rhythm & Drums

Steady, legible groove without excessive fills.

## Bass & Low End

Simple bass pattern that does not compete with cadence.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

One or two strong motifs, minimal harmonic changes.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Open vocal space; adaptable to many flows and rhyme densities.

## Arrangement

Long 8/16-bar cycles, subtle transitions, few disruptive drops.

## Mood & Energy

Focused, flexible, cypher-ready.

## Performance Detail

Protect vocal intelligibility: avoid busy midrange layers during dense verses and use instrumental accents at phrase endings.
