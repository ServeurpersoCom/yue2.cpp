# Trap

**Family:** Trap
**Typical tempo:** 120–160 BPM with half-time feel

## Core Identity

Modern hip-hop centered on 808 bass, rolling hi-hats, sparse melodic loops, and strong rhythmic negative space.

## Rhythm & Drums

Punchy kick, clap/snare on half-time backbeat, hat rolls, triplets, open-hat accents.

## Bass & Low End

Long 808s with slides, pitch movement, and strong sub presence.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Bells, plucks, pianos, pads, short minor-key motifs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rhythmic pocketing, triplet flows, melodic phrases or clipped delivery.

## Arrangement

Intro, hook, verse, hook with drops, mutes, risers, beat changes.

## Mood & Energy

Dark-to-polished, spacious, hard-hitting.

## Performance Detail

Treat the 808 as both bass and rhythmic counterpoint; leave intentional gaps so slides and drum accents remain clear.
