# Futuristic Hip-Hop

**Family:** Futuristic
**Typical tempo:** 70–160 BPM

## Core Identity

Forward-looking hip-hop using novel synthesis, advanced sound design, unexpected rhythms, and polished digital production.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hybrid acoustic/electronic hits, unusual percussion, precise programming.

## Bass & Low End

Synthetic bass, resampled 808s, modulated sub.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Granular synths, spectral textures, digital leads, unusual processing.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Flexible: robotic, melodic, rapid, processed, or clean.

## Arrangement

Nonstandard transitions, evolving sound design, beat switches.

## Mood & Energy

Innovative, digital, sleek, unpredictable.
