# G-Funk

**Family:** West Coast
**Typical tempo:** 82–100 BPM

## Core Identity

West Coast funk-derived hip-hop with elastic bass, bright synth leads, relaxed drums, and spacious melodic grooves.

## Rhythm & Drums

Laid-back swing, clap/snare backbeat, crisp hats, funk percussion.

## Bass & Low End

Prominent rubbery synth or electric bassline.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Whining synth leads, Parliament-style funk colors, electric piano, talkbox-like textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Smooth, relaxed, charismatic delivery; melodic hooks fit naturally.

## Arrangement

Extended grooves, instrumental intros/outros, hook-heavy structure.

## Mood & Energy

Sunny, cruising, confident, smooth.
