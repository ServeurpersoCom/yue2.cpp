# UK Drill

**Family:** Drill / Regional
**Typical tempo:** 138–145 BPM

## Core Identity

Polished, rhythmically intricate drill with sliding bass, syncopated kicks, eerie melodies, and sharply articulated flows.

## Rhythm & Drums

Complex kick placement, tight snare, fast hat patterns, percussion accents.

## Bass & Low End

Highly mobile sliding 808s with deliberate pitch choreography.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Dark piano, bells, strings, choir, sparse atmospheric samples.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Precise, clipped, percussive cadence with frequent rhythmic switches.

## Arrangement

Tension intro, verse-led arrangement, hook sections with strong bass movement.

## Mood & Energy

Cold, technical, threatening, cinematic.

## Performance Detail

The kick and 808 should interlock rather than constantly hit together; bass slides need deliberate pitch targets.
