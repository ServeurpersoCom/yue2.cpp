# Club Rap

**Family:** Club / Commercial
**Typical tempo:** 95–135 BPM

## Core Identity

Bass-forward rap optimized for loud playback, rhythmic impact, clear hooks, and repetitive movement.

## Rhythm & Drums

Large kicks, claps, syncopated percussion, clean transients.

## Bass & Low End

Sub-heavy, simple, powerful bass or 808.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Minimal synth hooks, stabs, vocal chops, risers.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Direct, rhythmic, hook-focused, chant-ready.

## Arrangement

Fast access to hook, drops, short verses, instrumental breaks.

## Mood & Energy

Physical, confident, high-impact, nightlife-focused.

## Performance Detail

Prioritize physical rhythm and fast recognition. Hooks should arrive quickly and low-end transients should remain uncluttered.
