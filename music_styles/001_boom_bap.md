# Boom Bap

**Family:** Classic / Sample-Based
**Typical tempo:** 82–96 BPM

## Core Identity

Classic sample-driven hip-hop built around hard kick/snare interplay, swung groove, chopped musical fragments, and a strong pocket for rapping.

## Rhythm & Drums

Punchy kick, cracking snare, dusty hats, light swing; drums should feel human rather than grid-perfect.

## Bass & Low End

Warm sampled bass, upright/electric bass fragments, or simple sub reinforcement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Chopped soul, jazz, funk, or soundtrack material; filtered loops, stabs, scratches, vinyl texture.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Bar-focused MC delivery; clear pocket, internal rhyme space, minimal melodic dependence.

## Arrangement

Loop-centered verses with subtle sample variation, drops, fills, and a stronger hook section.

## Mood & Energy

Head-nod, grounded, confident, raw.

## Performance Detail

Keep the groove human: slight swing and imperfect sample timing are usually more convincing than rigid quantization.
