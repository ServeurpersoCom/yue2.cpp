# Funk Rap

**Family:** Funk / Groove
**Typical tempo:** 85–110 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop centered on syncopated basslines, funk guitar, punchy drums, and danceable rhythmic interplay.

## Rhythm & Drums

Tight kick/snare groove, ghost notes, claps, percussion.

## Bass & Low End

Highly active electric or synth bassline.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Wah guitar, clavinet, brass, synth stabs, talkbox-style textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rhythmic, charismatic, playful or boastful delivery.

## Arrangement

Groove-driven sections, breakdowns, instrumental fills.

## Mood & Energy

Bouncy, confident, playful, physical.
