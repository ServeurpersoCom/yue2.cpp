# West Coast Hip-Hop

**Family:** Regional
**Typical tempo:** 80–102 BPM

## Core Identity

Broad West Coast sound emphasizing funk influence, spacious mixes, clear basslines, relaxed-to-hard drum grooves, and cinematic polish.

## Rhythm & Drums

Clean, open drums with strong backbeat and bounce.

## Bass & Low End

Defined bassline or sub; often more melodic than East Coast approaches.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Funk synths, pianos, strings, guitars, bells, atmospheric pads.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rhythmic, confident, often conversational delivery.

## Arrangement

Strong hooks, roomy verses, occasional instrumental breaks.

## Mood & Energy

Cruising, confident, polished, streetwise.
