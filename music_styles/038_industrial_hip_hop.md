# Industrial Hip-Hop

**Family:** Experimental / Heavy
**Typical tempo:** 70–130 BPM

## Core Identity

Abrasive hip-hop using distorted percussion, metallic textures, machine noise, and dense sound design.

## Rhythm & Drums

Clanging hits, distorted kicks, clipped snares, mechanical loops.

## Bass & Low End

Distorted bass, drones, sub pressure, noise-layered low end.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Metallic samples, feedback, synth noise, machinery, harsh processing.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Aggressive, detached, spoken, shouted, or heavily processed.

## Arrangement

Abrupt transitions, breakdowns, texture changes, unconventional song form.

## Mood & Energy

Harsh, dystopian, confrontational, mechanical.

## Performance Detail

Maintain one recognizable anchor—groove, motif, bass figure, or vocal rhythm—while other elements mutate around it.
