# Pop Rap

**Family:** Commercial / Pop
**Typical tempo:** 85–115 BPM or 120–160 half-time

## Core Identity

Accessible hip-hop with concise songwriting, polished production, strong hooks, and broad melodic appeal.

## Rhythm & Drums

Clean, punchy, radio-ready drums.

## Bass & Low End

Controlled bass/808, clear translation on small speakers.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Simple memorable chords, synths, guitar, bright motifs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Clear rap verses and highly memorable melodic hooks.

## Arrangement

Short intro, hook early, compact verses, bridge, final hook.

## Mood & Energy

Catchy, polished, energetic, emotionally direct.
