# PluggnB

**Family:** Trap / R&B
**Typical tempo:** 125–160 BPM

## Core Identity

Plugg-derived drums blended with lush R&B harmony, sparkling synths, and melodic vocal writing.

## Rhythm & Drums

Light trap percussion with soft attack and syncopated hats.

## Bass & Low End

Smooth 808 or sub that follows chord roots.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Rich seventh/ninth chords, glossy keys, bright leads, pads.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Melodic rap, stacked harmonies, soft falsetto or intimate delivery.

## Arrangement

Hook-heavy, fluid sections, layered vocal moments.

## Mood & Energy

Romantic, glossy, dreamy, emotional.

## Performance Detail

Treat the 808 as both bass and rhythmic counterpoint; leave intentional gaps so slides and drum accents remain clear.
