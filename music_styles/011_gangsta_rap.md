# Gangsta Rap

**Family:** Hard / Street
**Typical tempo:** 75–100 BPM

## Core Identity

Street-narrative hip-hop focused on realism, tension, power, consequence, and cinematic urban storytelling.

## Rhythm & Drums

Heavy but spacious; snare and kick should support narrative cadence.

## Bass & Low End

Deep bass, funk bass, or restrained 808 depending era.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Funk, soul, ominous keys, strings, synths, ambient street textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Calm menace, detailed storytelling, direct speech, controlled intensity.

## Arrangement

Narrative verses, memorable hooks, instrumental breathing room.

## Mood & Energy

Serious, cinematic, tense, streetwise.
