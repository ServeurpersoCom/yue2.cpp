# Brooklyn Drill

**Family:** Drill / Regional
**Typical tempo:** 138–150 BPM

## Core Identity

New York drill blending UK-influenced production with more forceful American vocal phrasing and energetic hooks.

## Rhythm & Drums

UK-drill-style kicks and hats with harder punch.

## Bass & Low End

Sliding 808s, often louder and more aggressive.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Dark pianos, brass, strings, vocal chops, ominous synths.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Commanding, energetic, punchline-heavy delivery.

## Arrangement

Hook-driven, compact sections, frequent drops and vocal callouts.

## Mood & Energy

Aggressive, energetic, urban, imposing.

## Performance Detail

The kick and 808 should interlock rather than constantly hit together; bass slides need deliberate pitch targets.
