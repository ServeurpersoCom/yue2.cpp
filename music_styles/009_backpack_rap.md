# Backpack Rap

**Family:** Underground / Lyrical
**Typical tempo:** 80–96 BPM

## Core Identity

Lyric-centered underground rap associated with crate-digging production, clever writing, and a deliberately non-commercial feel.

## Rhythm & Drums

Dusty boom-bap, crisp snare, swung hats.

## Bass & Low End

Sampled or warm electric bass.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Soul/jazz chops, obscure loops, scratches, simple melodic fragments.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Dense bars, wordplay, storytelling, technical rhyme schemes.

## Arrangement

Long verses, modest hooks, DJ-style transitions.

## Mood & Energy

Thoughtful, witty, grounded, underground.

## Performance Detail

Protect vocal intelligibility: avoid busy midrange layers during dense verses and use instrumental accents at phrase endings.
