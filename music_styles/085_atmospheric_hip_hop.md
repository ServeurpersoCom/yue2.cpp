# Atmospheric Hip-Hop

**Family:** Atmospheric
**Typical tempo:** 60–100 BPM

## Core Identity

Spacious hip-hop where ambience, reverb, texture, and slow-moving harmony are as important as the drums.

## Rhythm & Drums

Soft or restrained drums; long tails and spacious placement.

## Bass & Low End

Warm sub, low drone, or minimal bass.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Pads, reversed textures, field ambience, distant keys, vocal clouds.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Intimate, melodic, reflective, or detached delivery.

## Arrangement

Long intros/outros, gradual layer changes, restrained transitions.

## Mood & Energy

Immersive, reflective, nocturnal, spacious.
