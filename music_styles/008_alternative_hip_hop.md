# Alternative Hip-Hop

**Family:** Experimental / Alternative
**Typical tempo:** 70–110 BPM

## Core Identity

Broad genre-crossing hip-hop that avoids one fixed formula, combining rap with indie, electronic, rock, soul, jazz, or art-pop ideas.

## Rhythm & Drums

Can range from organic breakbeats to electronic kits; prioritize distinctive groove.

## Bass & Low End

Bass should fit the hybrid palette rather than default to 808s.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Unusual instruments, genre hybrids, left-field harmony, creative processing.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Flexible: melodic rap, spoken delivery, technical bars, or mixed vocal forms.

## Arrangement

More songlike or experimental structures; bridges and contrast sections encouraged.

## Mood & Energy

Individualistic, adventurous, expressive.

## Performance Detail

Maintain one recognizable anchor—groove, motif, bass figure, or vocal rhythm—while other elements mutate around it.
