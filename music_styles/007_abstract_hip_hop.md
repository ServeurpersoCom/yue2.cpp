# Abstract Hip-Hop

**Family:** Experimental / Alternative
**Typical tempo:** 65–100 BPM

## Core Identity

Non-literal, fragmented, or surreal hip-hop using unconventional rhythm, sound selection, and lyrical structure.

## Rhythm & Drums

Off-kilter placement, unusual percussion, abrupt silence, asymmetrical patterns.

## Bass & Low End

Can be minimal, distorted, unstable, or textural rather than melodic.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Micro-samples, noise, detuned keys, strange synths, collage techniques.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Associative imagery, unusual cadence, broken phrasing, spoken or deadpan delivery.

## Arrangement

Nonstandard sections, abrupt transitions, short loops, deliberate discontinuity.

## Mood & Energy

Surreal, cerebral, uneasy, inventive.

## Performance Detail

Maintain one recognizable anchor—groove, motif, bass figure, or vocal rhythm—while other elements mutate around it.
