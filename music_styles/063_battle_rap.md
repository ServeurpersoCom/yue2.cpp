# Battle Rap

**Family:** Performance / Lyrical
**Typical tempo:** 78–98 BPM

## Core Identity

Sparse, aggressive hip-hop designed to foreground punchlines, cadence changes, and verbal impact.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hard snare, simple kick pattern, minimal rhythmic clutter.

## Bass & Low End

Controlled low end; avoid bass masking the voice.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Dark stabs, tense strings/piano, minimal loops.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Highly articulate, confrontational, punchline-heavy, stop-start delivery.

## Arrangement

Long verses, strategic pauses, limited hook dependence.

## Mood & Energy

Competitive, tense, direct, forceful.

## Performance Detail

Protect vocal intelligibility: avoid busy midrange layers during dense verses and use instrumental accents at phrase endings.
