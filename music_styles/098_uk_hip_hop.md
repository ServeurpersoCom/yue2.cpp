# UK Hip-Hop

**Family:** UK / Regional
**Typical tempo:** 75–100 BPM

## Core Identity

British hip-hop outside grime/drill, often combining boom-bap, jazz, soul, spoken delivery, and distinctly UK vocal rhythm.

## Rhythm & Drums

Boom-bap or live-influenced drums; restrained swing.

## Bass & Low End

Warm bass or sample bass.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Soul/jazz samples, piano, strings, understated modern textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Clear UK cadence, storytelling, social observation, technical bars.

## Arrangement

Verse-forward with understated hooks and instrumental breathing room.

## Mood & Energy

Grounded, reflective, urban, lyrical.
