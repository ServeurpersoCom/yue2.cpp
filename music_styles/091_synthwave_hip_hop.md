# Synthwave Hip-Hop

**Family:** Futuristic / Retro
**Typical tempo:** 85–120 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop layered over 1980s-inspired synthwave harmony, gated drums, analog bass, arpeggios, and neon cinematic textures.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hip-hop groove blended with gated/snappy retro drums.

## Bass & Low End

Analog synth bass plus controlled sub.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Juno-style pads, arps, bright leads, gated reverb, retro keys.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Cool, melodic, cinematic, sometimes deadpan.

## Arrangement

Strong intro atmosphere, hook-driven sections, synth breakdown.

## Mood & Energy

Neon, nocturnal, nostalgic, cinematic.
