# Dirty South

**Family:** Southern / Street
**Typical tempo:** 70–100 BPM

## Core Identity

Raw Southern hip-hop emphasizing booming bass, chant energy, aggressive percussion, and club/street impact.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hard claps/snares, rolling hats, stomping kicks, syncopated percussion.

## Bass & Low End

Large sub or 808 with simple commanding movement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Dark synths, brass stabs, bells, minimal loops.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Bold, repetitive, chant-ready delivery with strong rhythmic phrasing.

## Arrangement

Big hooks, call-and-response, breaks for crowd energy.

## Mood & Energy

Rowdy, forceful, bass-heavy.
