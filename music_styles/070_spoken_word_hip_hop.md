# Spoken-Word Hip-Hop

**Family:** Lyrical / Poetic
**Typical tempo:** 55–90 BPM

## Core Identity

Minimal hip-hop supporting poetry-like delivery, dynamic speech rhythm, and emotional language rather than conventional rap cadence.

## Rhythm & Drums

Sparse percussion, brushes, rimshots, soft kicks, or no drums in sections.

## Bass & Low End

Subtle bass or drone.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Piano, strings, ambient textures, jazz harmony, field recordings.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Natural speech cadence, pauses, dynamic emphasis, intimate tone.

## Arrangement

Free-form sections, gradual builds, instrumental breathing room.

## Mood & Energy

Intimate, reflective, literary, emotionally exposed.

## Performance Detail

Protect vocal intelligibility: avoid busy midrange layers during dense verses and use instrumental accents at phrase endings.
