# Cinematic Hip-Hop

**Family:** Cinematic
**Typical tempo:** 60–110 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop arranged like a film score, using dynamic builds, orchestral or ambient layers, scene-setting textures, and dramatic pacing.

## Rhythm & Drums

Can move from minimal percussion to large impact drums.

## Bass & Low End

Sub/bass follows dramatic arc, sometimes absent in intros.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Strings, brass, piano, sound design, drones, impacts, ambience.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Narrative or emotionally dramatic delivery; space for spoken sections.

## Arrangement

Multi-stage builds, breakdowns, climaxes, transitions, motif reprises.

## Mood & Energy

Epic, dramatic, immersive, visual.

## Performance Detail

Use motifs and dynamic range. Avoid stacking every large sound at once; save the widest orchestration for structural peaks.
