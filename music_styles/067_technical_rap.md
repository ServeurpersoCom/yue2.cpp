# Technical Rap

**Family:** Lyrical / Technical
**Typical tempo:** 75–105 BPM or double-time

## Core Identity

Production tailored to intricate rhyme density, cadence shifts, breath control, and rhythmic precision.

## Rhythm & Drums

Very clear subdivision; crisp hats and snare provide timing landmarks.

## Bass & Low End

Tight low end with short decay.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Minimal motifs, dark keys, simple loops, occasional accent stabs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rapid internal rhyme, multi-syllabic patterns, tempo switches, precise articulation.

## Arrangement

Long verses with purposeful beat drops for emphasis.

## Mood & Energy

Controlled, intense, intricate, athletic.

## Performance Detail

Protect vocal intelligibility: avoid busy midrange layers during dense verses and use instrumental accents at phrase endings.
