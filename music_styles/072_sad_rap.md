# Sad Rap

**Family:** Melodic / Emotional
**Typical tempo:** 60–90 BPM or 120–150 half-time

## Core Identity

Melancholic rap built around sparse harmony, slow pacing, introspection, and subdued production.

## Rhythm & Drums

Soft, simple, low-impact drums.

## Bass & Low End

Rounded sub or quiet 808.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Piano, guitar, ambient pads, rain/room textures, minimal motifs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Low-energy, reflective, melodic or spoken delivery.

## Arrangement

Long verses, restrained hooks, gradual dynamic movement.

## Mood & Energy

Somber, introspective, lonely, bittersweet.
