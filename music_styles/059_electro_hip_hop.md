# Electro Hip-Hop

**Family:** Electronic / Old School
**Typical tempo:** 105–130 BPM

## Core Identity

Early electronic hip-hop language featuring drum machines, robotic synths, vocoder textures, and breakdance-oriented grooves.

## Rhythm & Drums

TR-style electronic drums, syncopated kicks, handclaps.

## Bass & Low End

Synth bass, sequenced low-end patterns.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Arpeggiators, vocoder, futuristic leads, electro bleeps.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rhythmic MCing, robotic processed phrases, call-and-response.

## Arrangement

Extended groove sections and instrumental breaks.

## Mood & Energy

Futuristic-retro, playful, mechanical, dance-oriented.
