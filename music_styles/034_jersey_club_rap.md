# Jersey Club Rap

**Family:** Club / Rap
**Typical tempo:** 135–155 BPM

## Core Identity

Rap over fast Jersey club rhythms, characterized by chopped kick patterns, repeated vocal edits, and dance-floor momentum.

## Rhythm & Drums

Rapid triplet/bounce kicks, claps, chopped percussion, rhythmic stop-starts.

## Bass & Low End

Short sub hits or bass pulses; rhythm takes priority over sustain.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Vocal chops, simple synth hooks, club samples.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Short, catchy, rhythmic phrases; delivery often mirrors kick pattern.

## Arrangement

Compact sections, quick transitions, repeated dance breaks.

## Mood & Energy

Kinetic, playful, club-focused, high-energy.

## Performance Detail

Prioritize physical rhythm and fast recognition. Hooks should arrive quickly and low-end transients should remain uncluttered.
