# Latin Hip-Hop

**Family:** Latin / Regional
**Typical tempo:** 80–110 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop integrating Latin rhythmic language, percussion, melodic instruments, and regional musical references.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hip-hop drums blended with congas, timbales, claves, shakers, or dembow-adjacent accents.

## Bass & Low End

Warm bass or sub with syncopation.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Guitar, brass, piano, regional percussion, sampled vocals.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rhythmic rap, bilingual phrasing where relevant, melodic hooks.

## Arrangement

Danceable verse/hook structure with percussion breaks.

## Mood & Energy

Rhythmic, warm, social, culturally textured.
