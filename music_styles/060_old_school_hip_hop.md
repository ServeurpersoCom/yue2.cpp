# Old-School Hip-Hop

**Family:** Classic / Early
**Typical tempo:** 95–115 BPM

## Core Identity

Foundational late-1970s/early-1980s hip-hop with break-driven grooves, party MCing, simple rhyme patterns, and DJ-centered structure.

## Rhythm & Drums

Funk/disco breaks, handclaps, simple drum-machine support.

## Bass & Low End

Funk/disco bassline, often continuous and danceable.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Breaks, rhythm guitar, horns, disco/funk loops, DJ cuts.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Call-and-response, rhythmic boasting, crowd interaction.

## Arrangement

Long groove-based sections with alternating MC passages.

## Mood & Energy

Joyful, communal, rhythmic, foundational.
