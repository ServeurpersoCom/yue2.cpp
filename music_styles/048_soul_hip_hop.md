# Soul Hip-Hop

**Family:** Soul / Sample-Based
**Typical tempo:** 72–96 BPM

## Core Identity

Warm hip-hop drawing from soul records, gospel harmony, expressive vocals, and emotionally rich chord movement.

## Rhythm & Drums

Classic boom-bap or relaxed modern drums.

## Bass & Low End

Warm sampled/electric bass; melodic root movement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Soul vocals, Rhodes, strings, guitar, organ, warm chord progressions.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Expressive, personal, reflective, confident; melodic hooks welcome.

## Arrangement

Verses supported by rich sample loops; hooks can open up harmonically.

## Mood & Energy

Warm, heartfelt, nostalgic, uplifting or bittersweet.
