# Latin Trap

**Family:** Latin / Trap
**Typical tempo:** 120–155 BPM

## Core Identity

Trap production with Latin melodic, rhythmic, and vocal sensibilities, combining 808 weight with regional harmonic and percussion colors.

## Rhythm & Drums

Trap hats/claps with Latin percussion accents.

## Bass & Low End

Sliding 808s and deep sub.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Guitar, piano, plucks, atmospheric synths, Latin rhythmic motifs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Melodic rap, rhythmic phrasing, sung hooks.

## Arrangement

Hook-forward with sparse verses and rhythmic breakdowns.

## Mood & Energy

Dark-to-romantic, smooth, rhythmic, modern.

## Performance Detail

Treat the 808 as both bass and rhythmic counterpoint; leave intentional gaps so slides and drum accents remain clear.
