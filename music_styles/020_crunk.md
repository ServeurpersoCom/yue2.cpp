# Crunk

**Family:** Southern / Club
**Typical tempo:** 70–90 BPM or double-time

## Core Identity

High-energy Southern club rap driven by shouted hooks, aggressive drums, repetitive synth motifs, and crowd-response dynamics.

## Rhythm & Drums

Huge kicks, claps, rapid hats, energetic percussion.

## Bass & Low End

Large 808/sub hits with simple patterns.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Aggressive synth stabs, sirens, brass-like patches, minimal harmony.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Shouted, chant-heavy, call-and-response vocals.

## Arrangement

Short verses, huge repeated hooks, breakdowns and drops.

## Mood & Energy

Explosive, rowdy, physical, communal.

## Performance Detail

Prioritize physical rhythm and fast recognition. Hooks should arrive quickly and low-end transients should remain uncluttered.
