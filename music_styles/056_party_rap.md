# Party Rap

**Family:** Club / Commercial
**Typical tempo:** 90–120 BPM

## Core Identity

Upbeat hip-hop designed for group energy, memorable slogans, simple rhythmic hooks, and immediate dancefloor response.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hard kicks, claps, bright percussion, danceable swing.

## Bass & Low End

Strong bass pulse without overcomplication.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Bright synths, brass stabs, vocal chops, simple riffs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Chants, call-and-response, short memorable phrases.

## Arrangement

Frequent hooks, drops, breakdowns, crowd-response sections.

## Mood & Energy

Fun, social, energetic, uncomplicated.

## Performance Detail

Prioritize physical rhythm and fast recognition. Hooks should arrive quickly and low-end transients should remain uncluttered.
