# Horrorcore

**Family:** Dark / Theatrical
**Typical tempo:** 65–100 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop framed with horror imagery, ominous cinematic sound design, unsettling harmony, and theatrical vocal character.

## Rhythm & Drums

Heavy, sparse, often boom-bap or trap-derived.

## Bass & Low End

Dark sub, low drones, sampled bass.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Horror-score strings, detuned piano, choirs, bells, sound effects.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Narrative, theatrical, whispered-to-aggressive dynamics.

## Arrangement

Cinematic intros, story verses, dramatic hooks, tension breaks.

## Mood & Energy

Macabre, theatrical, suspenseful, sinister.
