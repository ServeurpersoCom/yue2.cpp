# Storytelling Rap

**Family:** Lyrical / Narrative
**Typical tempo:** 70–98 BPM

## Core Identity

Narrative-first hip-hop where musical choices support scene changes, pacing, tension, character, and emotional resolution.

## Rhythm & Drums

Steady drums with dynamic changes tied to narrative moments.

## Bass & Low End

Bass follows emotional arc rather than dominating.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Cinematic samples, piano, strings, atmosphere, location-like sound design.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Clear diction, varied tone, character voices, controlled pacing.

## Arrangement

Verses behave like scenes; transitions may mark time/place changes.

## Mood & Energy

Cinematic, immersive, reflective, dramatic.

## Performance Detail

Protect vocal intelligibility: avoid busy midrange layers during dense verses and use instrumental accents at phrase endings.
