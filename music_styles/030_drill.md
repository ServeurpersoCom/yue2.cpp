# Drill

**Family:** Drill
**Typical tempo:** 135–150 BPM

## Core Identity

Dark street rap defined by sliding bass, syncopated kick patterns, sparse minor-key melodies, and tense vocal cadence.

## Rhythm & Drums

Off-grid-feeling kicks, sharp snares, rapid hats, percussive syncopation.

## Bass & Low End

Gliding 808s with pitch bends and long slides.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Dark piano, bells, strings, choirs, sparse ambience.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Clipped, rhythmically precise, confrontational delivery.

## Arrangement

Short intros, verse-heavy structure, hooks with strong rhythmic repetition.

## Mood & Energy

Tense, cold, menacing, urgent.

## Performance Detail

The kick and 808 should interlock rather than constantly hit together; bass slides need deliberate pitch targets.
