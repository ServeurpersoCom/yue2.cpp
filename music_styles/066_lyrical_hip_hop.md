# Lyrical Hip-Hop

**Family:** Lyrical
**Typical tempo:** 75–100 BPM

## Core Identity

Rap-centered production deliberately leaves space for complex writing, rhyme schemes, and nuanced vocal rhythm.

## Rhythm & Drums

Clear pocket, moderate density, strong snare reference.

## Bass & Low End

Simple supportive bass.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Restrained sample or melodic loop with limited frequency clutter.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Dense bars, internal rhymes, multi-syllabic patterns, dynamic cadence.

## Arrangement

Verse-dominant, hooks simple enough not to distract.

## Mood & Energy

Focused, intelligent, expressive, performance-first.

## Performance Detail

Protect vocal intelligibility: avoid busy midrange layers during dense verses and use instrumental accents at phrase endings.
