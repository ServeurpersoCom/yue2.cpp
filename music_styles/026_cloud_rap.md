# Cloud Rap

**Family:** Atmospheric
**Typical tempo:** 70–140 BPM depending drum feel

## Core Identity

Dreamlike hip-hop using washed textures, airy pads, soft drums, and impressionistic vocals.

## Rhythm & Drums

Soft trap or lo-fi drums, smeared transients, airy hats.

## Bass & Low End

Rounded sub or floating 808, usually not overly aggressive.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Reverb-heavy pads, dream synths, vocal textures, shimmering samples.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Mumbled, melodic, detached, or softly layered delivery.

## Arrangement

Long atmospheric intros/outros, simple repeated sections.

## Mood & Energy

Ethereal, hazy, introspective, weightless.
