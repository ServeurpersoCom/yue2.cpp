# Downtempo Hip-Hop

**Family:** Downtempo / Chill
**Typical tempo:** 60–85 BPM

## Core Identity

Slow, spacious hip-hop designed around relaxed grooves, warm texture, and understated melodic movement.

## Rhythm & Drums

Soft drums, delayed percussion, loose swing.

## Bass & Low End

Rounded bass, low dynamic aggression.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Pads, keys, guitar, mellow samples, ambient layers.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Relaxed rap or sparse vocal presence.

## Arrangement

Gradual development, long loops, smooth transitions.

## Mood & Energy

Calm, reflective, spacious, late-night.
