# Gospel Rap

**Family:** Soul / Spiritual
**Typical tempo:** 70–105 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop that uses gospel musical language—choirs, organ, uplifting harmony, communal dynamics—within a rap song framework.

## Rhythm & Drums

Can be boom-bap, trap, live drums, or handclap-driven.

## Bass & Low End

Warm bass, organ-supported low end, or 808 depending modernity.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Choir, Hammond organ, piano, handclaps, strings, major/extended harmony.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Earnest, declarative, inspirational; sung choir hooks fit strongly.

## Arrangement

Builds toward larger choruses, call-and-response, dynamic lifts.

## Mood & Energy

Uplifting, reverent, triumphant, communal.
