# Epic Hip-Hop

**Family:** Cinematic / Anthemic
**Typical tempo:** 70–110 BPM or 140–160 half-time

## Core Identity

Large-scale hip-hop emphasizing huge drums, dramatic harmonic movement, broad synth/orchestral layers, and climactic hooks.

## Rhythm & Drums

Massive kicks/snares, impact drums, percussion builds.

## Bass & Low End

Powerful sub/808, often reinforced with orchestral lows.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Brass, strings, choir, synth stacks, risers, impacts.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Commanding, motivational, anthem-ready delivery.

## Arrangement

Build/drop/climax structure with repeated peak hook.

## Mood & Energy

Triumphant, intense, monumental, motivational.

## Performance Detail

Use motifs and dynamic range. Avoid stacking every large sound at once; save the widest orchestration for structural peaks.
