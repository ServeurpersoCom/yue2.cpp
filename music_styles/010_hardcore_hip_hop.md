# Hardcore Hip-Hop

**Family:** Hard / Street
**Typical tempo:** 78–102 BPM

## Core Identity

Aggressive hip-hop emphasizing forceful drums, confrontational energy, dense low end, and commanding vocal presence.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hard kicks, sharp snares, heavy percussion, minimal softness.

## Bass & Low End

Thick bass or sub with aggressive envelope.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Dark samples, minor keys, stabs, sirens, tense strings, distorted layers.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Forceful projection, clipped phrasing, high conviction.

## Arrangement

Strong verse/hook contrast, breakdowns, dramatic stops.

## Mood & Energy

Intense, confrontational, muscular.
