# French Hip-Hop

**Family:** Regional / European
**Typical tempo:** 75–155 BPM

## Core Identity

Broad French rap aesthetic spanning sample-heavy boom-bap, cinematic orchestration, dark street rap, and polished modern trap.

## Rhythm & Drums

Depends era: boom-bap, trap, or cinematic drums.

## Bass & Low End

Sample bass, sub, or 808 according to substyle.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Piano, strings, chanson/soul samples, dark synths, orchestral elements.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Highly rhythmic syllabic delivery; strong emphasis on phrasing and internal rhyme.

## Arrangement

Verse-led with memorable refrain; cinematic transitions common.

## Mood & Energy

Serious, lyrical, cinematic, streetwise.
