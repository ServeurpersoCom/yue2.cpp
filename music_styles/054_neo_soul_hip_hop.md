# Neo-Soul Hip-Hop

**Family:** Soul / Jazz
**Typical tempo:** 68–95 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop with neo-soul harmony, live-feeling instrumentation, deep pocket, and sophisticated chord voicings.

## Rhythm & Drums

Loose pocket, ghost notes, rimshots, brushed textures.

## Bass & Low End

Warm electric/upright bass with melodic movement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Rhodes, guitar, organ, jazz extensions, live keys, subtle strings.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Conversational rap, soulful singing, understated dynamics.

## Arrangement

Organic evolution, instrumental bridges, layered harmonies.

## Mood & Energy

Warm, intimate, sophisticated, reflective.
