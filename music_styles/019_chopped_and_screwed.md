# Chopped & Screwed

**Family:** Southern / Experimental
**Typical tempo:** 45–70 BPM perceived

## Core Identity

A slow, psychedelic remix aesthetic built from dramatically reduced tempo, repeated fragments, pitch shifts, and stuttered edits.

## Rhythm & Drums

Original groove slowed; edits and retriggers become part of rhythm.

## Bass & Low End

Pitch-lowered low end, long sub tails, thickened bass texture.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Stretched samples, tape-like drag, repeated words, abrupt rewind effects.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Vocals pitched down and extended; phrases can repeat rhythmically.

## Arrangement

Nonlinear remix structure with cuts, repeats, drops, and slowed transitions.

## Mood & Energy

Dazed, heavy, hypnotic, dreamlike.

## Performance Detail

Maintain one recognizable anchor—groove, motif, bass figure, or vocal rhythm—while other elements mutate around it.
