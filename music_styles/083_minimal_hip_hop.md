# Minimal Hip-Hop

**Family:** Minimal
**Typical tempo:** 65–105 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop reduced to a few essential elements so rhythm, vocal character, and negative space carry the track.

## Rhythm & Drums

Sparse kick/snare/hat pattern; every hit matters.

## Bass & Low End

One simple bass or 808 figure.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Single motif, texture, stab, or small chord loop.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Vocal is foregrounded; pauses and rhythmic nuance become important.

## Arrangement

Few layers, strong muting, subtle section changes.

## Mood & Energy

Focused, cool, dry, modern.
