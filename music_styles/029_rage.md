# Rage

**Family:** Trap / Electronic
**Typical tempo:** 130–165 BPM

## Core Identity

Hyper-energetic rap built on distorted synth leads, huge 808s, bright digital textures, and repetitive festival-like momentum.

## Rhythm & Drums

Fast hats, hard claps, aggressive kicks, sparse but forceful patterns.

## Bass & Low End

Large clipped or distorted 808s with simple movement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Saw leads, arpeggios, video-game-like synths, supersaws, bright distortion.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Repetitive, shouted, melodic or chant-based delivery.

## Arrangement

Short build, explosive drop, repeated hook, minimal breakdown.

## Mood & Energy

Chaotic, euphoric, aggressive, futuristic.

## Performance Detail

Treat the 808 as both bass and rhythmic counterpoint; leave intentional gaps so slides and drum accents remain clear.
