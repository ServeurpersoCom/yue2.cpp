# Soulful Boom Bap

**Family:** Boom Bap / Soul
**Typical tempo:** 78–96 BPM

## Core Identity

Boom-bap with warm soul samples, expressive chord movement, vocal chops, and emotionally resonant grooves.

## Rhythm & Drums

Punchy but warm, modest swing.

## Bass & Low End

Warm bass sample or electric bass.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Soul vocals, strings, Rhodes, guitar, organ.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Reflective, confident, heartfelt delivery.

## Arrangement

Verses use chopped loop; hooks open up with fuller sample phrase.

## Mood & Energy

Warm, nostalgic, triumphant or bittersweet.

## Performance Detail

Keep the groove human: slight swing and imperfect sample timing are usually more convincing than rigid quantization.
