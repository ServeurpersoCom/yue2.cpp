# Glitch Hop

**Family:** Electronic / Experimental
**Typical tempo:** 85–115 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop/electronic hybrid using digital stutters, chopped edits, syncopated bass, and highly processed rhythmic detail.

## Rhythm & Drums

Punchy electronic drums with micro-edits, fills, retriggers, syncopation.

## Bass & Low End

Elastic synth bass, wobble, or glitchy low-end movement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Digital clicks, resampling, stutter edits, chopped synths.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rhythmic rap or sampled vocals; vocals may be heavily edited.

## Arrangement

Drop-oriented but groove-centered, with frequent micro-variation.

## Mood & Energy

Futuristic, playful, technical, kinetic.

## Performance Detail

Maintain one recognizable anchor—groove, motif, bass figure, or vocal rhythm—while other elements mutate around it.
