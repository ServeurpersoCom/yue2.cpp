# Reggae Hip-Hop

**Family:** Caribbean / Fusion
**Typical tempo:** 72–95 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop fused with reggae’s offbeat rhythm, dub bass, spacious effects, and relaxed vocal pocket.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hip-hop drums with one-drop/steppers influence, rimshots, offbeat accents.

## Bass & Low End

Deep dub-style bassline is central.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Skank guitar/keys, organ bubble, dub delay, horns.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Relaxed rap, singjay-style melody, call-and-response.

## Arrangement

Verse/hook with dub breakdowns and delay throws.

## Mood & Energy

Laid-back, warm, conscious or celebratory.
