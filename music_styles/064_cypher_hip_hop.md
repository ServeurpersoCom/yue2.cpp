# Cypher Hip-Hop

**Family:** Performance / Lyrical
**Typical tempo:** 80–96 BPM

## Core Identity

Loop-centered hip-hop designed for multiple MCs to trade verses while maintaining a stable groove and neutral vocal space.

## Rhythm & Drums

Consistent boom-bap or stripped groove; modest fills.

## Bass & Low End

Simple looped bass that stays out of the midrange.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Sample loop, keys, horns, or restrained atmosphere.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Multiple distinct flows; beat should support handoffs between rappers.

## Arrangement

Extended verse blocks, short reset transitions, optional refrain.

## Mood & Energy

Communal, focused, energetic, lyrical.

## Performance Detail

Protect vocal intelligibility: avoid busy midrange layers during dense verses and use instrumental accents at phrase endings.
