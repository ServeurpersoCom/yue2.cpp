# Grimy Boom Bap

**Family:** Boom Bap / Dark
**Typical tempo:** 78–94 BPM

## Core Identity

A dirtier, more abrasive boom-bap variant with rough sampling, hard drums, low-mid weight, and street-level atmosphere.

## Rhythm & Drums

Crushed kicks, snapping snares, gritty hats, hard swing.

## Bass & Low End

Dirty sampled bass or saturated low-end loop.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Dark soul/jazz chops, detuned piano, brass, vinyl grit.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Forceful, bar-heavy, dry vocal presence.

## Arrangement

Long verse loops, abrupt mutes, sample drops, DJ cuts.

## Mood & Energy

Dirty, hard, urban, uncompromising.

## Performance Detail

Keep the groove human: slight swing and imperfect sample timing are usually more convincing than rigid quantization.
