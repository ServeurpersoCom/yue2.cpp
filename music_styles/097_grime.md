# Grime

**Family:** UK / Electronic
**Typical tempo:** 138–142 BPM

## Core Identity

Fast UK MC music built around sparse 140-BPM electronic production, aggressive square-wave bass, and sharply rhythmic delivery.

## Rhythm & Drums

Sparse punchy drums, syncopated snares, electronic percussion.

## Bass & Low End

Square-wave/sub bass, often simple and aggressive.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Cold synths, eski-style bleeps, strings, metallic textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rapid, percussive, confrontational MC flow with strong cadence.

## Arrangement

8/16-bar loops, quick switches, minimal hook dependence.

## Mood & Energy

Cold, urgent, raw, competitive.
