# Drift Phonk

**Family:** Electronic / Aggressive
**Typical tempo:** 120–170 BPM

## Core Identity

High-energy phonk derivative built for driving momentum, distorted low end, repetitive cowbell leads, and hard compression.

## Rhythm & Drums

Fast hard kicks, sharp snares, driving hats.

## Bass & Low End

Heavily distorted bass/808, often clipped for aggression.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Prominent cowbell melody, dark synth layers, impact FX.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Vocals are usually chopped or secondary to the instrumental.

## Arrangement

Build/drop structure, strong repeated lead motif.

## Mood & Energy

Aggressive, mechanical, adrenaline-heavy.
