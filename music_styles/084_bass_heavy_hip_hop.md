# Bass-Heavy Hip-Hop

**Family:** Bass / Modern
**Typical tempo:** 65–150 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop designed around dominant low-frequency impact while retaining enough headroom and midrange space for drums and vocals.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hard, simple drums with clear transient separation.

## Bass & Low End

Primary feature: large sub, 808, synth bass, slides or sustained notes.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Minimal mids; sparse motifs prevent masking.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Direct, rhythmically strong delivery that can cut through dense low end.

## Arrangement

Drops and mutes emphasize bass arrival; hooks maximize low-end impact.

## Mood & Energy

Physical, powerful, dark or club-ready.
