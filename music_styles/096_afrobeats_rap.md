# Afrobeats Rap

**Family:** African / Fusion
**Typical tempo:** 95–115 BPM

## Core Identity

Rap integrated with Afrobeats production: syncopated percussion, melodic guitar/keys, smooth bass, and hook-led songwriting.

## Rhythm & Drums

Layered shakers, rimshots, kick syncopation, percussion loops.

## Bass & Low End

Warm rounded bass with melodic movement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Clean guitars, plucks, keys, bright pads, vocal textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Melodic rap, sing-rap, smooth phrasing, catchy hooks.

## Arrangement

Short verses, recurring hook, danceable instrumental sections.

## Mood & Energy

Smooth, warm, infectious, uplifting.
