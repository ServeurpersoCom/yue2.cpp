# Dream Rap

**Family:** Atmospheric / Dreamy
**Typical tempo:** 65–145 BPM

## Core Identity

Ethereal rap using soft percussion, shimmering harmony, blurred textures, and emotionally distant or surreal vocal presentation.

## Rhythm & Drums

Soft trap/lo-fi drums, airy hats, gentle transients.

## Bass & Low End

Smooth sub/808 with little distortion.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Dream pads, chorus guitar, bells, reversed textures, reverb clouds.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Breathy, melodic, detached, layered, occasionally whispered.

## Arrangement

Simple hooks, floating verse sections, long ambience tails.

## Mood & Energy

Dreamlike, wistful, weightless, surreal.
