# Dancehall Rap

**Family:** Caribbean / Fusion
**Typical tempo:** 90–110 BPM

## Core Identity

Rap over dancehall-influenced rhythms with syncopated drums, heavy bass, percussive vocal cadence, and energetic hooks.

## Rhythm & Drums

Dembow/dancehall-informed kick-snare patterns, percussion, shakers.

## Bass & Low End

Heavy but rhythmic bass, often short and punchy.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Sparse synth stabs, plucks, vocal chops, tropical percussion.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Highly rhythmic, melodic or chant-like delivery.

## Arrangement

Hook-heavy, dance-focused, quick transitions.

## Mood & Energy

Energetic, confident, warm, club-ready.
