# Fast Rap / Chopper

**Family:** Lyrical / Technical
**Typical tempo:** 85–110 BPM with double-time vocal feel

## Core Identity

High-speed rap format designed for double-time or extremely dense syllabic delivery while preserving intelligibility.

## Rhythm & Drums

Simple, crisp, highly legible drums; avoid clutter at fast subdivisions.

## Bass & Low End

Short controlled bass notes or 808s.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Minimal harmonic material; rhythmic stabs can mark phrase boundaries.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Very fast articulation, repeated rhythmic cells, breath-aware phrase design.

## Arrangement

Sections should include brief rests, drops, or half-time resets.

## Mood & Energy

Energetic, technical, urgent, precision-focused.

## Performance Detail

Protect vocal intelligibility: avoid busy midrange layers during dense verses and use instrumental accents at phrase endings.
