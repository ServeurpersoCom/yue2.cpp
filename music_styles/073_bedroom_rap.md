# Bedroom Rap

**Family:** Lo-Fi / Indie
**Typical tempo:** 65–100 BPM

## Core Identity

DIY, intimate hip-hop that embraces imperfect recording texture, simple arrangements, and personal songwriting.

## Rhythm & Drums

Loose programmed drums, room-like texture, low-fi transients.

## Bass & Low End

Simple bass or sub; not overly polished.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Home-recorded guitar/keys, cheap synths, room noise, tape saturation.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Close, conversational, understated, sometimes imperfect by design.

## Arrangement

Compact, songlike forms with handmade transitions.

## Mood & Energy

Personal, warm, awkward-in-a-good-way, intimate.

## Performance Detail

Use degradation selectively. The mix can sound worn without becoming muddy; preserve a readable kick, snare, and vocal range.
