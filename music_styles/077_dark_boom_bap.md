# Dark Boom Bap

**Family:** Boom Bap / Dark
**Typical tempo:** 76–94 BPM

## Core Identity

Boom-bap built around ominous samples, minor harmony, sparse melodic motion, and cinematic tension.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hard but controlled; snare-forward groove.

## Bass & Low End

Deep sampled bass or sub reinforcement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Minor piano, horror/suspense strings, low brass, eerie vocal chops.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Measured, serious, technically precise rap.

## Arrangement

Loop-focused with tension variations and dramatic drops.

## Mood & Energy

Nocturnal, sinister, focused, cinematic.

## Performance Detail

Keep the groove human: slight swing and imperfect sample timing are usually more convincing than rigid quantization.
