# Experimental Hip-Hop

**Family:** Experimental
**Typical tempo:** Variable 60–160 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop that intentionally breaks conventions in rhythm, timbre, structure, or vocal design while preserving a rap-centered identity.

## Rhythm & Drums

Unusual meters, polymetric feel, broken grooves, unconventional samples.

## Bass & Low End

May be sub-heavy, absent, distorted, or rhythmically fragmented.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Any palette: synthesis, found sound, granular texture, live instruments, noise.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Nonstandard flow, spoken passages, pitch processing, abrupt cadence shifts.

## Arrangement

Unpredictable sections, asymmetry, beat switches, silence as arrangement.

## Mood & Energy

Inventive, challenging, surprising, boundary-pushing.

## Performance Detail

Maintain one recognizable anchor—groove, motif, bass figure, or vocal rhythm—while other elements mutate around it.
