# Trap Soul

**Family:** Trap / R&B
**Typical tempo:** 120–155 BPM half-time feel

## Core Identity

Emotional hybrid of trap drums and R&B songwriting, with lush harmony, intimate vocals, and restrained low-end power.

## Rhythm & Drums

Soft trap hats, snaps/claps, restrained kick patterns.

## Bass & Low End

Smooth 808/sub, usually warm rather than abrasive.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Rhodes, guitar, pads, extended chords, ambient vocal textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Sung-rap, layered harmonies, intimate lead, emotionally nuanced phrasing.

## Arrangement

Verse/pre-hook/hook, bridges, vocal breakdowns.

## Mood & Energy

Moody, sensual, introspective, polished.

## Performance Detail

Treat the 808 as both bass and rhythmic counterpoint; leave intentional gaps so slides and drum accents remain clear.
