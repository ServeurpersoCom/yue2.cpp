# Afro Hip-Hop

**Family:** African / Fusion
**Typical tempo:** 85–115 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop blended with African rhythmic traditions, guitar/keyboard patterns, layered percussion, and regional groove concepts.

## Rhythm & Drums

Polyrhythmic percussion, shakers, claps, syncopated kick patterns.

## Bass & Low End

Warm melodic bass, sometimes less sub-heavy than trap.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Guitars, marimba-like plucks, keys, horns, regional textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rhythmic rap, melodic refrains, call-and-response.

## Arrangement

Groove-led structure with instrumental movement.

## Mood & Energy

Warm, rhythmic, communal, vibrant.
