# R&B Rap

**Family:** R&B / Melodic
**Typical tempo:** 70–100 BPM or 130–160 half-time

## Core Identity

Hip-hop blended with smooth contemporary R&B harmony, polished drums, melodic hooks, and intimate vocal production.

## Rhythm & Drums

Soft punchy drums, trap/R&B hats, clean snaps.

## Bass & Low End

Smooth sub or 808 kept controlled.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Electric piano, pads, guitar, lush chords, vocal textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rap verses with sung hooks, melodic phrasing, close-mic intimacy.

## Arrangement

Strong verse/pre-hook/hook structure, bridges common.

## Mood & Energy

Smooth, romantic, polished, emotional.
