# Miami Bass

**Family:** Bass / Regional
**Typical tempo:** 110–135 BPM

## Core Identity

Fast Southern dance rap built around booming sub-bass, drum-machine patterns, syncopated rhythms, and party energy.

## Rhythm & Drums

808 drum machine, rapid kicks, claps, cowbell/percussion accents.

## Bass & Low End

Very deep sustained or pulsing sub-bass.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Sparse synth stabs, electro textures, vocal chants.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rhythmic, energetic, repetitive, party-focused.

## Arrangement

Loop-driven with breakdowns designed for dancers.

## Mood & Energy

Loud, playful, bass-dominant, energetic.

## Performance Detail

Prioritize physical rhythm and fast recognition. Hooks should arrive quickly and low-end transients should remain uncluttered.
