# Retro-Futuristic Hip-Hop

**Family:** Futuristic / Retro
**Typical tempo:** 80–130 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop imagining the future through vintage synth technology, analog textures, early digital sounds, and modern drum weight.

## Rhythm & Drums

Modern punch with retro drum-machine accents.

## Bass & Low End

Synth bass combining analog warmth with modern sub.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Analog polysynths, FM keys, vintage arps, tape effects.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Smooth, stylish, rhythmic delivery; melodic hooks fit well.

## Arrangement

Verse/hook with synth interludes and retro transitions.

## Mood & Energy

Nostalgic-futurist, stylish, cinematic, playful.
