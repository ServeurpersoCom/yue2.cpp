# Sampledelic Hip-Hop

**Family:** Sample-Based / Experimental
**Typical tempo:** 75–110 BPM

## Core Identity

Dense collage-style hip-hop built from many manipulated samples, layered references, unusual juxtapositions, and evolving edits.

## Rhythm & Drums

Breakbeats layered with chopped percussion and found rhythms.

## Bass & Low End

Sample-derived bass or resampled low-end fragments.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Multiple micro-samples, vocal snippets, records, film fragments, resampling.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rap can be sparse or rhythmically integrated into the collage.

## Arrangement

Constant detail changes while preserving a central groove.

## Mood & Energy

Playful, surreal, dense, crate-digger-oriented.

## Performance Detail

Maintain one recognizable anchor—groove, motif, bass figure, or vocal rhythm—while other elements mutate around it.
