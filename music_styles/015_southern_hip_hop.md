# Southern Hip-Hop

**Family:** Regional
**Typical tempo:** 65–95 BPM or double-time feel

## Core Identity

Broad Southern rap language built on heavy low end, strong rhythmic identity, regional bounce, and flexible tempos.

## Rhythm & Drums

808-forward, rolling hats, claps, syncopated percussion, bounce variations.

## Bass & Low End

Deep sub and long 808 notes; bass can carry melodic movement.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Sparse synths, bells, pianos, soul elements, atmospheric motifs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Flexible cadence, drawl-friendly pocket, rhythmic repetition, melodic hooks.

## Arrangement

Hook-forward with clear drops and energetic transitions.

## Mood & Energy

Heavy, confident, hypnotic, regional.
