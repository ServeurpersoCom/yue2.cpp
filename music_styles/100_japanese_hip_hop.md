# Japanese Hip-Hop

**Family:** Regional / Asian
**Typical tempo:** 70–155 BPM

## Core Identity

Broad Japanese hip-hop palette ranging from jazz-rich sample production to minimal modern trap and experimental electronic rap.

## Rhythm & Drums

Boom-bap, jazzy breaks, or clean trap drums depending direction.

## Bass & Low End

Warm sampled bass or modern 808.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Jazz chords, city-pop fragments, keys, synths, atmospheric textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Precise rhythmic phrasing; can be conversational, melodic, or technical.

## Arrangement

Clean, deliberate sections with strong production detail.

## Mood & Energy

Refined, rhythmic, atmospheric, stylistically flexible.
