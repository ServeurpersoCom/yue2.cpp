# Instrumental Hip-Hop

**Family:** Beat / Instrumental
**Typical tempo:** 65–105 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop where the beat itself carries the narrative through sample development, groove, texture, and arrangement rather than vocals.

## Rhythm & Drums

Any hip-hop drum language; groove must remain compelling without an MC.

## Bass & Low End

Bass should provide melodic/rhythmic identity.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Samples, live instruments, synths, chops, turntablism, textures.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

No lead vocal required; vocal chops can function as instruments.

## Arrangement

More frequent variation, beat changes, breakdowns, and instrumental motifs.

## Mood & Energy

Depends on substyle; prioritize musical storytelling.
