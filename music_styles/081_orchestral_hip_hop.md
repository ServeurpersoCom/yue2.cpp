# Orchestral Hip-Hop

**Family:** Cinematic
**Typical tempo:** 70–105 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop built around orchestral instrumentation and arrangement while retaining a strong rap pocket.

## Rhythm & Drums

Hip-hop drums layered with timpani, orchestral percussion, cymbal swells.

## Bass & Low End

Deep bass plus low strings/brass support.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Strings, brass, woodwinds, choir, piano, orchestral ostinatos.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Commanding, cinematic, lyrical or narrative delivery.

## Arrangement

Clear builds, thematic reprises, orchestral interludes.

## Mood & Energy

Grand, serious, dramatic, stately.

## Performance Detail

Use motifs and dynamic range. Avoid stacking every large sound at once; save the widest orchestration for structural peaks.
