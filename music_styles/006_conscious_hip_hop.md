# Conscious Hip-Hop

**Family:** Lyrical / Message
**Typical tempo:** 72–98 BPM

## Core Identity

Message-centered rap where production supports clarity, reflection, social commentary, personal growth, or philosophical themes.

## Rhythm & Drums

Supportive rather than overpowering; clean boom-bap, soul, or modern drums.

## Bass & Low End

Stable, musical low end that leaves vocal room.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Soul, piano, strings, restrained samples, atmospheric pads.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Highly intelligible, emotionally controlled, lyric-forward delivery.

## Arrangement

Verses carry narrative/argument; hooks summarize emotional or thematic center.

## Mood & Energy

Reflective, purposeful, humane, serious.

## Performance Detail

Protect vocal intelligibility: avoid busy midrange layers during dense verses and use instrumental accents at phrase endings.
