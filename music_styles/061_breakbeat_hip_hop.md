# Breakbeat Hip-Hop

**Family:** Classic / Breaks
**Typical tempo:** 85–115 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop emphasizing chopped or looped drum breaks, syncopated funk percussion, and rhythmic sample manipulation.

## Rhythm & Drums

Breaks are the centerpiece; preserve ghost notes and human swing.

## Bass & Low End

Bass should lock tightly to break accents.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Funk/soul samples, percussion loops, short stabs.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Rhythmic rap that rides changing drum accents.

## Arrangement

Break variations, fills, cuts, drum-only sections.

## Mood & Energy

Groovy, kinetic, sample-driven.
