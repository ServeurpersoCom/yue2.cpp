# Phonk

**Family:** Memphis-Derived / Electronic
**Typical tempo:** 80–130 BPM

## Core Identity

Modernized Memphis-inspired hip-hop using lo-fi vocal samples, cowbells, distorted bass, and dark retro texture.

## Rhythm & Drums

Dry drums, cowbell rhythm, crunchy snares, trap-influenced hats.

## Bass & Low End

Distorted sub/808 with saturated mids.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Cowbells, Memphis vocal cuts, horror textures, vinyl/tape effects.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Often sample-led; if original vocals are used, keep them sparse and gritty.

## Arrangement

Loop-driven, short sections, heavy repetition.

## Mood & Energy

Dark, retro, hypnotic, gritty.
