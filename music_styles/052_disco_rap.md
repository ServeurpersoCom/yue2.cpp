# Disco Rap

**Family:** Funk / Dance
**Typical tempo:** 100–125 BPM

## Core Identity

Hip-hop blended with disco grooves, four-on-the-floor influence, bright strings, rhythm guitar, and dancefloor bass.

## Rhythm & Drums

Four-on-the-floor or disco-inflected kick with claps and open hats.

## Bass & Low End

Moving octave bassline or disco-funk bass.

## Harmony, Melody & Sound Palette

Strings, piano, rhythm guitar, bright synths, handclaps.

## Vocal / Rap Approach

Party-oriented rhythmic rap, chant hooks, crowd interaction.

## Arrangement

Verse/hook with dance breaks and extended groove sections.

## Mood & Energy

Celebratory, bright, glamorous, danceable.

## Performance Detail

Prioritize physical rhythm and fast recognition. Hooks should arrive quickly and low-end transients should remain uncluttered.
